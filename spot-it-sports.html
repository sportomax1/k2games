Game guide

How to play

Each round shows two circular cards. Exactly one team logo appears on both. Select that logo on either card to score a match.

  • ALL 6: Mix every league. Each card includes as many different leagues as its chosen logo count allows.
  • CORE 4: NFL, NBA, MLB and NHL only.
  • Single league: Pick NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, college football (CFB) or Division I men’s college basketball (CBB). CFB includes ESPN-mapped FBS and FCS teams; CBB uses the 2025–26 Division I field.
  • Difficulty: 4, 6, or 8 logos on each card. More logos make matching harder.
  • Timer: Score as many matches as you can in 30–120 seconds. Wrong taps count as misses and cost 1 second.
  • Fair timer: The countdown pauses briefly while the next pair of logos is prepared, and you can manually pause the game.

High scores are saved in this browser separately for each league, difficulty and timer combination. Logo images need internet access; abbreviations are used if they cannot load.
